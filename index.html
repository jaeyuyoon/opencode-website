Harbor Stargazers Club

Look up with us

Harbor Stargazers is a fictional student club for anyone who wants to learn the night sky. No telescope and no experience needed. We meet twice a month, share snacks, and spend an hour finding planets, constellations, and the occasional satellite.

When and where we meet

  • First Friday: Beginner night on the rooftop deck, 8:00 PM
  • Third Friday: Open sky night on the campus lawn, 8:30 PM
  • Cloudy weather: We move indoors for a star map and trivia session

What to bring

  • A warm jacket, even in early fall
  • A phone with a free sky map app, set to red night mode
  • A friend, since stargazing is better with company

Join the club

Fill out our one-minute interest form at the next meeting, or just show up on a Friday. Everyone is welcome.

Harbor Stargazers Club is invented for a class assignment.
